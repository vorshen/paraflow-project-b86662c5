# Home Page
The main landing page that showcases featured technical articles, provides navigation to different content categories, and introduces users to TechFlow's curated technology insights.

Layout Hierarchy:
- Header (Full-width):
  - Top Navigation Bar
- Content Container (Positioned below the header):
  - Main Content Area
- Footer (Full-width):
  - Footer Links and Information

## Top Navigation Bar
- TechFlow brand logo
- Categories dropdown menu
- Search functionality with expandable search bar
- User authentication buttons (Sign In/Sign Up)
- Dark mode toggle

## Hero Section
- Featured article spotlight with large image and headline
- Brief article summary and "Read More" call-to-action
- Author information and publication date
- Category tags

## Featured Articles
- Grid of 6-8 curated articles with thumbnails
- Article titles with compelling headlines
- Author names and avatars
- Publication dates and reading time estimates
- Category tags and engagement indicators

## Category Navigation
- Technology category cards (Frontend, Backend, DevOps, AI/ML, Mobile, Security)
- Category descriptions and article counts
- Visual icons representing each technology area
- Quick access links to category pages

## Community Highlights
- Recent popular discussions preview
- Top contributors showcase
- Trending topics and tags
- Community engagement statistics

## Newsletter Subscription
- Email subscription form with compelling value proposition
- Featured benefits of subscribing
- Privacy assurance and subscription frequency information

## Footer
- About TechFlow links
- Contributor guidelines and submission process
- Social media links
- Contact information and support
- Privacy policy and terms of service
